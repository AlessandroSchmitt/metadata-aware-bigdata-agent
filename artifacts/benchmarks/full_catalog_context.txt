=== SEMANTIC CONCEPTS ===
- pickup_datetime [temporal]: Date and time when a taxi trip begins. | aliases=pickup time, trip start
- dropoff_datetime [temporal]: Date and time when a taxi trip ends. | aliases=dropoff time, trip end
- pickup_zone_id [geographic]: TLC taxi-zone identifier for the trip pickup location. | aliases=pickup location
- dropoff_zone_id [geographic]: TLC taxi-zone identifier for the trip drop-off location. | aliases=dropoff location
- taxi_zone_id [geographic]: Primary TLC identifier of a taxi zone.
- zone_name [geographic]: Human-readable TLC taxi-zone name. | aliases=taxi zone
- borough [geographic]: NYC borough associated with a TLC taxi zone. | aliases=borough name
- trip_distance [mobility]: Recorded distance of a taxi trip in miles. | aliases=distance
- passenger_count [mobility]: Recorded number of passengers for a taxi trip. | aliases=passengers
- fare_amount [monetary]: Base fare amount associated with a taxi trip.
- tip_amount [monetary]: Tip amount associated with a taxi trip.
- total_amount [monetary]: Total recorded monetary amount associated with a taxi trip.
- weather_hour [temporal]: Clock-hour bucket represented by an hourly weather record.
- temperature [weather]: Observed dry-bulb air temperature in degrees Fahrenheit. | aliases=temperature
- relative_humidity [weather]: Observed relative humidity percentage. | aliases=humidity
- precipitation_amount [weather]: Numeric hourly precipitation amount in inches when measurable. | aliases=rainfall
- precipitation_trace [weather]: Indicates trace precipitation that is present but lacks a measurable numeric amount.
- precipitation_status [weather]: Three-valued precipitation status: true means precipitation occurred, false means dry, null means unknown. | aliases=rain, rainy, wet weather
- wind_speed [weather]: Observed wind speed in miles per hour. | aliases=wind
- visibility [weather]: Observed visibility in miles. | aliases=visibility
- hour_of_day [temporal]: Integer clock hour of day from 0 to 23, independent of calendar date. | aliases=clock hour, clock hour of day, hour of day

=== CROSS-SOURCE RELATIONSHIPS ===
- yellow_pickup_zone [foreign_key_like, many_to_one, validated]: yellow_taxi.PULocationID = taxi_zones.LocationID
  meaning: Maps each Yellow Taxi pickup LocationID to its TLC taxi-zone record.
- yellow_dropoff_zone [foreign_key_like, many_to_one, validated]: yellow_taxi.DOLocationID = taxi_zones.LocationID
  meaning: Maps each Yellow Taxi drop-off LocationID to its TLC taxi-zone record.
- green_pickup_zone [foreign_key_like, many_to_one, validated]: green_taxi.PULocationID = taxi_zones.LocationID
  meaning: Maps each Green Taxi pickup LocationID to its TLC taxi-zone record.
- green_dropoff_zone [foreign_key_like, many_to_one, validated]: green_taxi.DOLocationID = taxi_zones.LocationID
  meaning: Maps each Green Taxi drop-off LocationID to its TLC taxi-zone record.
- yellow_pickup_weather_hour [temporal_alignment, many_to_one, validated]: date_trunc('hour', yellow_taxi.tpep_pickup_datetime) = weather_hourly.weather_hour
  meaning: Aligns a Yellow Taxi pickup timestamp with the corresponding hourly NYC weather bucket.
- green_pickup_weather_hour [temporal_alignment, many_to_one, validated]: date_trunc('hour', green_taxi.lpep_pickup_datetime) = weather_hourly.weather_hour
  meaning: Aligns a Green Taxi pickup timestamp with the corresponding hourly NYC weather bucket.

=== SEMANTIC SQL RULES ===
- rainy_hour [weather_hourly]: weather_hourly.has_precipitation = TRUE
  meaning: An hour is rainy when NOAA indicates measurable or trace precipitation.
  result_semantics: Includes trace precipitation. Missing weather is not classified as rainy.
- dry_hour [weather_hourly]: weather_hourly.has_precipitation = FALSE
  meaning: An hour is dry only when precipitation status is explicitly false.
  result_semantics: Missing precipitation status is not interpreted as dry.
- yellow_pickup_weather_bucket [yellow_taxi]: date_trunc('hour', yellow_taxi.tpep_pickup_datetime)
  meaning: Hourly timestamp bucket for a Yellow Taxi pickup, used for alignment with hourly weather.
  result_semantics: Returns a timestamp truncated to the hour. It is not the integer clock hour of day.
- green_pickup_weather_bucket [green_taxi]: date_trunc('hour', green_taxi.lpep_pickup_datetime)
  meaning: Hourly timestamp bucket for a Green Taxi pickup, used for alignment with hourly weather.
  result_semantics: Returns a timestamp truncated to the hour. It is not the integer clock hour of day.
- yellow_pickup_hour_of_day [yellow_taxi]: hour(yellow_taxi.tpep_pickup_datetime)
  meaning: Extracts the integer clock hour of day from a Yellow Taxi pickup timestamp.
  result_semantics: Returns an integer from 0 to 23. Use this for grouping pickups by clock hour across different dates.
- green_pickup_hour_of_day [green_taxi]: hour(green_taxi.lpep_pickup_datetime)
  meaning: Extracts the integer clock hour of day from a Green Taxi pickup timestamp.
  result_semantics: Returns an integer from 0 to 23. Use this for grouping pickups by clock hour across different dates.
